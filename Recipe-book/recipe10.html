Gulab Jamun

Ingredients

  1. 1 cup milk powder
  2. 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  3. 1/4 tsp baking soda
  4. 2 tbsp ghee (clarified butter)
  5. 2-3 tbsp milk (as needed for the dough)
  6. Oil or ghee for deep frying
  7. 1 cup sugar
  8. 1 cup water
  9. 1/4 tsp cardamom powder
  10. 1 tsp rose water (optional)
  11. Few strands of saffron (optional)
  12. Chopped nuts for garnish (optional)

Instructions

  1. In a large mixing bowl, combine milk powder, all-purpose flour, and baking soda. Mix well.
  2. Add ghee and mix until the mixture resembles breadcrumbs.
  3. Gradually add milk, 1 tablespoon at a time, and mix to form a smooth and soft dough. Do not over knead.
  4. Cover the dough and let it rest for 15 minutes.
  5. Meanwhile, prepare the sugar syrup. In a pan, add sugar and water. Heat until the sugar dissolves completely and the syrup comes to a boil.
  6. Add cardamom powder, rose water, and saffron strands (if using) to the syrup. Simmer for a few more minutes, then remove from heat and set aside.
  7. Divide the dough into small equal-sized balls, ensuring there are no cracks.
  8. Heat oil or ghee in a deep frying pan over medium-low heat. The oil should be hot but not smoking.
  9. Gently slide the dough balls into the hot oil. Fry them in batches on low heat, stirring occasionally, until they turn golden brown and evenly cooked.
  10. Remove the fried balls with a slotted spoon and drain the excess oil.
  11. Immediately immerse the fried balls into the warm sugar syrup. Let them soak for at least 1-2 hours.
  12. Garnish with chopped nuts before serving. Serve warm or at room temperature.
