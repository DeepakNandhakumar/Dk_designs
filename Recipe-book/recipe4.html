Bread Halwa

Ingredients

  1. 6 slices of bread
  2. 1/2 cup ghee
  3. 1/2 cup sugar
  4. 1 cup milk
  5. 1/4 tsp cardamom powder
  6. 2 tbsp chopped nuts (cashews, almonds, etc.)
  7. 2 tbsp raisins

Instructions

  1. Remove the edges of the bread slices and cut them into small pieces.
  2. Heat 1/4 cup ghee in a pan and fry the bread pieces until they are golden brown. Remove and set aside.
  3. In the same pan, add the remaining ghee and fry the chopped nuts and raisins until they are golden brown. Remove and set aside.
  4. Add the milk to the pan and bring it to a boil. Add the sugar and stir until it dissolves.
  5. Add the fried bread pieces to the milk mixture and cook on low heat, stirring continuously, until the bread absorbs the milk and the mixture thickens.
  6. Add the cardamom powder and mix well.
  7. Garnish with the fried nuts and raisins. Serve warm.
