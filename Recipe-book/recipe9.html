Sakkarai Pongal

Ingredients

  1. 1 cup raw rice
  2. 1/4 cup moong dal (yellow split gram)
  3. 1 cup jaggery (grated)
  4. 3 cups water
  5. 1 cup milk
  6. 1/4 tsp cardamom powder
  7. 2 tbsp ghee
  8. 2 tbsp cashews
  9. 1 tbsp raisins
  10. A pinch of edible camphor (optional)

Instructions

  1. Wash the rice and moong dal together and drain.
  2. In a pressure cooker, add the rice, moong dal, and 3 cups of water. Cook for 4-5 whistles or until soft and mushy.
  3. In a separate pan, dissolve the grated jaggery in a little water and bring to a boil. Strain to remove impurities.
  4. Add the jaggery syrup to the cooked rice and dal mixture. Mix well.
  5. Add the milk, cardamom powder, and edible camphor (if using). Cook on low heat, stirring continuously, until the mixture thickens.
  6. In a small pan, heat the ghee and fry the cashews and raisins until golden brown. Add to the pongal and mix well.
  7. Serve hot.
