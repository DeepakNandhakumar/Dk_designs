Mutton Biryani

Ingredients

  1. 500g mutton (cut into pieces)
  2. 2 cups basmati rice
  3. 3 tbsp oil or ghee
  4. 2 large onions (sliced)
  5. 2 tomatoes (chopped)
  6. 1 cup yogurt
  7. 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  8. 2 green chilies (slit)
  9. 1 tsp turmeric powder
  10. 1 tbsp red chili powder
  11. 1 tbsp coriander powder
  12. 1 tsp garam masala
  13. 1 tsp cumin seeds
  14. 1 tsp fennel seeds
  15. 4 cloves
  16. 2 cardamom pods
  17. 1 cinnamon stick
  18. 1 bay leaf
  19. Fresh coriander leaves (chopped)
  20. Fresh mint leaves (chopped)
  21. Salt to taste
  22. 4 cups water
  23. Juice of 1 lemon
  24. Few saffron strands soaked in 2 tbsp warm milk (optional)
  25. Fried onions for garnish (optional)

Instructions

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice in water for 30 minutes. Drain and set aside.
  2. Heat oil or ghee in a large pot. Add cumin seeds, fennel seeds, cloves, cardamom pods, cinnamon stick, and bay leaf. Sauté until fragrant.
  3. Add the sliced onions and sauté until golden brown.
  4. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chilies. Sauté until the raw smell disappears.
  5. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until they become soft and mushy.
  6. Add turmeric powder, red chili powder, coriander powder, and salt. Mix well.
  7. Add the mutton pieces and cook until they are browned on all sides.
  8. Add yogurt and mix well. Cook for a few minutes until the yogurt is well incorporated.
  9. Add chopped coriander and mint leaves. Mix well.
  10. Add 4 cups of water and bring to a boil. Reduce the heat, cover, and simmer until the mutton is tender.
  11. In a separate pot, bring water to a boil. Add the soaked and drained basmati rice. Cook until the rice is 70% cooked. Drain and set aside.
  12. Once the mutton is tender, layer the partially cooked rice over the mutton mixture in the pot.
  13. Drizzle lemon juice and saffron milk (if using) over the rice. Cover with a tight-fitting lid and cook on low heat (dum) for 20-25 minutes.
  14. Remove from heat and let it rest for 10 minutes. Gently fluff the rice with a fork.
  15. Garnish with fried onions (if using) and serve hot with raita or a side salad.
