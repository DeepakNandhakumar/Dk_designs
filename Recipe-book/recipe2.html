Chicken 65

Ingredients

  1. 500g chicken (cut into bite-sized pieces)
  2. 2 tbsp yogurt
  3. 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  4. 1 tsp red chili powder
  5. 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  6. 1 tsp garam masala
  7. 1 tbsp lemon juice
  8. 2 tbsp corn flour
  9. 2 tbsp rice flour
  10. Salt to taste
  11. Oil for deep frying
  12. Few curry leaves
  13. 2 green chilies (slit)
  14. 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
  15. 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
  16. Fresh coriander leaves (for garnish)

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, mix yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric powder, garam masala, lemon juice, corn flour, rice flour, and salt. Add the chicken pieces and mix well to coat. Marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a deep frying pan. Fry the marinated chicken pieces in batches until golden brown and crispy. Drain on paper towels.
  3. In a separate pan, heat a little oil. Add mustard seeds and cumin seeds. Let them splutter.
  4. Add curry leaves and green chilies. Sauté for a few seconds.
  5. Add the fried chicken pieces to the pan and toss well to coat with the seasoning.
  6. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve hot as a starter or with rice and curry.
