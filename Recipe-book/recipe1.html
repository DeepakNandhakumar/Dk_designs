Meen Kozhambu

Ingredients

  1. 500g fish (cut into pieces)
  2. 2 tbsp oil
  3. 1 tsp mustard seeds
  4. 1 tsp fenugreek seeds
  5. 1 tsp cumin seeds
  6. 1 large onion (finely chopped)
  7. 2 tomatoes (finely chopped)
  8. 2 tbsp tamarind pulp
  9. 2 cups water
  10. 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  11. 1 tsp turmeric powder
  12. 2 tbsp red chili powder
  13. 1 tbsp coriander powder
  14. Salt to taste
  15. Few curry leaves
  16. Fresh coriander leaves (for garnish)

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a pan. Add mustard seeds, fenugreek seeds, and cumin seeds. Let them splutter.
  2. Add the chopped onions and sauté until golden brown.
  3. Add the ginger-garlic paste and sauté until the raw smell disappears.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until they become soft and mushy.
  5. Add turmeric powder, red chili powder, coriander powder, and salt. Mix well.
  6. Add the tamarind pulp and water. Bring it to a boil.
  7. Once it starts boiling, add the fish pieces and curry leaves. Reduce the heat and let it simmer until the fish is cooked and the gravy thickens.
  8. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve hot with steamed rice.
