Chicken Nuggets

Ingredients

  1. 500g boneless chicken breast (cut into bite-sized pieces)
  2. 1 cup all-purpose flour
  3. 1 tsp garlic powder
  4. 1 tsp onion powder
  5. 1/2 tsp paprika
  6. Salt and pepper to taste
  7. 2 large eggs (beaten)
  8. 2 cups bread crumbs (preferably panko)
  9. Oil for deep frying

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, combine the flour, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, salt, and pepper. Mix well.
  2. Place the beaten eggs in a separate bowl.
  3. Place the bread crumbs in another bowl.
  4. Coat each chicken piece in the seasoned flour mixture, then dip it in the beaten eggs, and finally coat it with bread crumbs. Make sure each piece is well coated.
  5. Heat oil in a deep frying pan over medium-high heat. To check if the oil is ready, drop a small piece of bread crumb into the oil. If it sizzles and rises to the surface, the oil is ready.
  6. Carefully place the coated chicken pieces in the hot oil. Fry in batches to avoid overcrowding the pan.
  7. Fry the chicken nuggets until they are golden brown and cooked through, about 5-6 minutes per batch. Use a slotted spoon to remove them from the oil and place them on paper towels to drain the excess oil.
  8. Serve hot with your favorite dipping sauce. Enjoy your homemade chicken nuggets!
