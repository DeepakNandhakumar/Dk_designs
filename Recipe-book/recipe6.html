Mysore Pak

Ingredients

  1. 1 cup gram flour (besan)
  2. 1 cup ghee
  3. 1 cup sugar
  4. 1/2 cup water

Instructions

  1. Grease a plate with ghee and set aside.
  2. Heat ghee in a pan until fully melted and hot.
  3. In another pan, mix sugar and water and bring to a boil. Cook until the sugar syrup reaches one-string consistency.
  4. Reduce the heat and gradually add the gram flour to the sugar syrup, stirring continuously to avoid lumps.
  5. Slowly pour the hot ghee into the mixture, stirring continuously until the mixture starts to leave the sides of the pan and becomes porous.
  6. Pour the mixture onto the greased plate and spread evenly.
  7. Let it cool slightly, then cut into desired shapes. Allow it to cool completely before serving.
