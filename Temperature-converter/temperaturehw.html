Temperature Conversion

Temperature can be converted between different units using formulas. The most common conversions are between Celsius (°C) and Fahrenheit (°F).

Formulas

  • Celsius to Fahrenheit: (°C × 9/5) + 32 = °F
  • Fahrenheit to Celsius: (°F - 32) × 5/9 = °C

Differences

Celsius and Fahrenheit are two different scales for measuring temperature. Here are some key differences:

  • The Celsius scale is used in most of the world and is based on the freezing and boiling points of water (0°C and 100°C, respectively).
  • The Fahrenheit scale is primarily used in the United States and its territories and is based on a different set of reference points (32°F for the freezing point of water and 212°F for the boiling point).
