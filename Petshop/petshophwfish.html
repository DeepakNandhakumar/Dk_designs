Fish

Fish are fascinating pets that add beauty and tranquility to any home aquarium. They come in various colors, shapes, and sizes, making them popular choices for pet enthusiasts.

Types of Fish

There are many types of fish suitable for keeping as pets. Some popular types include:

  • Goldfish: Known for their bright colors and easy care, goldfish are a classic choice for beginners.
  • Betta Fish: Also known as Siamese fighting fish, bettas are colorful and have distinct personalities. They are best kept alone in their own tank.
  • Guppies: Small and colorful, guppies are easy to care for and are known for their lively behavior.
  • Tetras: Tetras come in various colors and are peaceful community fish, ideal for larger aquariums.
  • Angelfish: Known for their elegant appearance, angelfish are larger freshwater fish that require spacious tanks.
